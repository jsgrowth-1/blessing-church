Who We Are

Blessing Church began with a handful of families praying in a living room, believing God had more for this town than Sunday routine. That conviction still shapes everything we do.

Today we're a growing, multi-generational community that gathers to worship honestly, study scripture seriously, and care for one another practically — through seasons of celebration and seasons of struggle alike.

Mission

To help people meet Jesus, grow in faith, and live out their purpose in everyday life.

Vision

A church where every generation — from toddlers to grandparents — finds a place to belong and grow.

Core Values

Scripture first. Prayer always. Generosity without condition. Community over performance.
